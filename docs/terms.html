Terms

Last updated 16 November 2026

These are the rules for using earshot. By making an account you agree to them. They are short on purpose.

Who can use earshot

You must be 18 or older.

One person, one account. Keep your login to yourself; what happens on your account is yours to answer for.

What is not allowed

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behaviour. That means: harassing, threatening or bullying anyone; hateful content about who people are; sexual content or advances nobody asked for, and anything sexual involving a minor; spam, scams or selling; pretending to be someone else; sharing someone’s private information; anything illegal.

This applies everywhere you can write or choose something: your name, handle and bio, song notes, chat messages, your room, guestbook signatures, city posts, threads and replies.

Do not try to work out or reveal where another person is. earshot is built so that nobody can, and trying is a breach of these terms.

What happens if you break the rules

Anyone can report you, and reports are looked at within 24 hours.

Content that breaks these rules is removed, and the account that posted it can be suspended or deleted, without warning.

If you see something that breaks the rules, report it from inside the app or write to earshot.support@gmail.com.

Your things

What you write and post stays yours. By posting it you allow earshot to store it and show it to other people in the ways the app describes.

You can delete it. Deleting your account removes everything attached to it, as the privacy page sets out.

The music

Song details, artwork and 30-second previews come from Apple’s iTunes catalogue and belong to their owners. earshot does not host or sell music.

The usual

earshot is provided as it is. It may change, break or stop, and it comes with no guarantee that it will always be available or free of faults.

You can stop using earshot and delete your account at any time. earshot can end or suspend an account that breaks these terms.

If these terms change, the date at the top changes with them. Carrying on using earshot after a change means you accept it.

Questions go to earshot.support@gmail.com.
